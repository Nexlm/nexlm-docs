# Incident playbook

## Horizon outage or slowness

**Symptoms:** users see "The Stellar network is unreachable", trades pile up in `Locking escrow`, `Releasing XLM` or `Refunding seller`, logs show `Horizon request failed`.

1. Check [Stellar status](https://status.stellar.org) and your Horizon provider.
2. Don't manually change trade statuses — reconciliation settles them once Horizon responds.
3. Post a notice to users that trades may take longer.
4. If the outage is prolonged, switch `HORIZON_URL` to another provider on the same network and restart.
5. Afterwards, filter **Trades** by the transitional statuses and confirm the list drains.

## Payment windows expiring during an outage

Auto-refunds also need Horizon, so they queue up and run when it's back. Buyers who paid but couldn't mark paid should contact support with their trade ID and receipt.

## Suspected fraud ring

1. Search **Users** for shared payout account numbers or similar names.
2. Review their trades' chats and receipts.
3. Suspend accounts — active orders are cancelled automatically; in-progress trades continue.
4. Record evidence before banning.

## Platform key exposure

Treat as critical.

1. Put the platform in maintenance (stop the API) to prevent new escrows.
2. Generate a new platform key.
3. For each open escrow, settle trades while the old key still controls them.
4. Deploy the new `PLATFORM_SECRET_KEY` and restart.
5. Review Stellar history of all escrow accounts for unexpected operations.

## Email delivery failures

Registration still succeeds; users can use **Resend email**. Check SMTP credentials and provider dashboards. Verification links can also be generated by updating `emailVerifyTokenHash` in the database in an emergency.
