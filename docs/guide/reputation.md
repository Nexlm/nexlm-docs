# Reputation

Your reputation appears on your offers, in trade rooms and on your public profile.

## Completed trades

The number of trades you took part in (as buyer or seller) that ended **Completed**.

## Completion rate

```
completion rate = completed trades ÷ (completed + cancelled trades)
```

Shown as a percentage with one decimal place. New traders with no closed trades show **—** instead of 0%.

## Verified badge

A badge next to a display name means the trader passed [identity verification](./identity-verification).

## Public profile

Tap any display name to open their profile at `/u/<displayName>`. It shows:

- Verified badge and join date
- Completed trades and completion rate
- Up to 10 of their active orders

Email addresses and payout details are never shown on profiles.
