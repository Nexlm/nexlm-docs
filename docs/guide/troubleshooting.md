# Troubleshooting

## "Verify your email address before trading"

Click the link in your verification email, or resend it from the banner. See [Verify your email](./verify-email).

## "Complete identity verification (BVN or NIN) before trading"

Submit your ID on the [identity verification](./identity-verification) page.

## "Add a payout account for … before posting a sell order"

Save an account for each payment method on the order in **Settings → Payout accounts**.

## "Insufficient XLM"

Each sell order and trade needs the amount **plus 2 XLM**, and your other active sell orders count too. Deposit more XLM or cancel an order. See [Balances explained](./balances).

## "Another trader just took this order"

Someone matched the order a moment before you. Pick another offer.

## "Finish your open trades first"

You can have at most 5 trades in progress. Complete or cancel some first.

## "This trade just changed. Refresh and try again."

The other party acted at the same time as you. The page refreshes with the latest status.

## "The Stellar network is unreachable right now"

Horizon, the Stellar API, didn't respond. Wait a minute and retry. Trades caught mid-operation are reconciled automatically.

## "Too many requests"

You hit a rate limit. Wait a few minutes.

## "Your session has expired"

Log in again. Sessions last 7 days.

## Balance looks wrong

Tap **Refresh** on the Wallet page. Balances are read live from Stellar.
