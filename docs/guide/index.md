# Welcome to Nexlm

Nexlm is a peer-to-peer (P2P) marketplace where people in Nigeria buy and sell **XLM** (Stellar Lumens) directly with each other for **Naira**.

- **Buyers** pay Naira straight into the seller's bank or mobile wallet account.
- **Sellers'** XLM is locked in an on-chain escrow account before any money moves.
- **Nexlm** matches traders, runs the escrow, and never holds your Naira.

## How a trade works

1. A seller posts an offer, or a buyer posts a request.
2. The other side opens a trade. The seller's XLM moves into a brand-new Stellar escrow account.
3. The buyer sends Naira within **15 minutes** and taps **I have paid**.
4. The seller confirms the money arrived and **releases** the escrow.
5. The XLM lands in the buyer's Nexlm wallet in about five seconds.

If the buyer doesn't pay in time, the escrow refunds the seller automatically.

::: tip Testnet during early access
Early access runs on the Stellar **testnet**. Test XLM has no value, and new wallets are funded with 10,000 test XLM automatically. Never send real XLM to a testnet address.
:::
