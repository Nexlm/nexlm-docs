# Manage your orders

**My Orders** lists everything you've posted. Filter by status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Visible on the market |
| **Matched** | Someone opened a trade on it |
| **Expired** | 30 minutes passed without a match |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled it, or your account was restricted |

## Cancel an order

Tap **Cancel** next to an active order. You can't cancel an order that was just matched — the trade has already started.

## When a matched order becomes active again

If the trade on your order is cancelled or the buyer's payment window expires, the order returns to **Active** as long as it hasn't passed its expiry time.

## Editing

Orders can't be edited yet. Cancel and post a new one instead. Editing and extending orders is on the roadmap.
