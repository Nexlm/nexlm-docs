# Staying safe

## Golden rules

1. **Sellers:** release only after the Naira is in your account — check your banking app, not an SMS or screenshot.
2. **Buyers:** tap **I have paid** only after the transfer has gone through.
3. Pay from, and receive into, accounts **in your own name**.
4. Keep every conversation in the **Nexlm trade chat**.
5. Never share your password or OTP. Nexlm staff will never ask.

## Transfer narration

Leave the narration blank or use something neutral. Don't mention crypto, XLM or Nexlm.

## Check your counterparty

- Prefer traders with a verified badge, completed trades and a high completion rate.
- Read the order terms before opening a trade.

## Protect your account

- Use a unique password you don't use elsewhere.
- Log out on shared devices.
- Verify links go to the real Nexlm app before logging in.

See [Common scams](./scams) for specific tricks to watch for.
