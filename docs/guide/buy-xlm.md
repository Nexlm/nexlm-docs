# Buy XLM

## 1. Pick a sell order

On the market's **Buy XLM** tab, choose an offer. Check the seller's reputation and terms first.

## 2. Open the trade

Tap **Buy XLM**, choose a payment method (if the seller accepts several) and confirm.

Nexlm checks that:

- the order is still available and isn't yours
- the seller has a payout account for that method and enough XLM
- you have fewer than 5 trades in progress

Then the seller's XLM is locked in escrow and you're taken to the [trade room](./trade-room).

## 3. Pay

Send the exact Naira total to the account shown in the trade room within **15 minutes**.

## 4. Tap "I have paid"

Confirm in the dialog. The timer stops and the seller is notified. Share your receipt in the [chat](./chat).

## 5. Receive XLM

When the seller [releases](./release) the escrow, the XLM arrives in your Nexlm wallet. The trade shows **Completed** with a link to the release transaction.

::: warning
Only tap **I have paid** after the transfer has actually left your account. False payment claims lead to suspension.
:::

## If something goes wrong

- **Can't pay in time?** [Cancel](./cancel-trade) before the window closes, or let it expire — the seller is refunded automatically.
- **Paid but the seller is quiet?** Keep your receipt in the chat and contact support with the trade ID. After you mark the trade paid, the XLM can no longer be refunded to the seller automatically.
