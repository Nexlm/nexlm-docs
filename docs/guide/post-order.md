# Post an order

Tap **Post an order** on the market.

## Choose a side

- **I want to sell XLM** — creates a sell order that appears on buyers' **Buy XLM** tab.
- **I want to buy XLM** — creates a buy order that appears on sellers' **Sell XLM** tab.

## Fill in the details

| Field | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| Amount | 10 – 100,000 XLM, up to 7 decimal places |
| Your price | Naira per XLM, up to 2 decimal places |
| Payment methods | At least one |
| Terms | Optional, up to 500 characters |

The form previews the Naira total.

## Extra checks for sell orders

- You need a [payout account](./payout-accounts) for **every** payment method you select.
- Your available balance must cover this order plus all your other active sell orders, each with a 2 XLM escrow overhead.

## Limits

- Up to **10 active orders** at a time
- Orders expire **30 minutes** after posting
- The whole order is filled by a single trade (partial fills are planned)

## Writing good terms

Keep them short and specific, e.g. *"Own-name accounts only. Exact amount in one transfer. Upload receipt after paying."* Don't ask traders to move to WhatsApp or Telegram.
