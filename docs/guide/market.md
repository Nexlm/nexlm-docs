# The P2P market

The **P2P Market** is the home page of the app. Anyone can browse it; you need an account to trade.

## Buy XLM vs Sell XLM

| Tab | Shows | Sorted by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buy XLM** | Sell orders from people selling XLM | Lowest price first |
| **Sell XLM** | Buy orders from people buying XLM | Highest price first |

Orders with the same price are shown oldest first.

## Filters

- **Min. amount** — only show orders of at least this many XLM
- **Payment method** — only show orders that accept a given method

## Reading an offer

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Advertiser | Display name, verified badge, completed trades and completion rate |
| Price | Naira per 1 XLM |
| Amount | XLM in the order, with the Naira total |
| Payment | Accepted payment methods |

Orders expire **30 minutes** after posting and disappear when matched, cancelled or expired. The board updates in real time while you're logged in; use **Refresh** otherwise.

::: tip
A "—" completion rate means the trader hasn't closed any trades yet.
:::
