# Create an account

1. Open the Nexlm app and choose **Create account**.
2. Enter your email, a display name and a password.
3. Submit. You're logged in straight away and taken to your wallet.

## Requirements

| Field | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| Email | A valid address you can access. Stored in lowercase. |
| Display name | 3–24 characters: letters, numbers and underscores. Must be unique (case-insensitive). Shown to other traders. |
| Password | At least 8 characters, with at least one letter and one number. |

## What happens behind the scenes

- A **Stellar keypair** is generated for your wallet. The secret key is encrypted before it's stored.
- On testnet, the wallet is funded with test XLM via Friendbot.
- A verification email is sent to your address.

::: warning Choose your display name carefully
Your display name is tied to your trading history and can't be changed in the app.
:::
