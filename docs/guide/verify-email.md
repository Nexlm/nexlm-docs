# Verify your email

After registering, Nexlm emails you a verification link. Click it to confirm your address.

A verified email is required before you can:

- post orders or open trades
- withdraw XLM

## Didn't get the email?

- Check your spam or promotions folder.
- In the app, the yellow **Verify your email address** banner has a **Resend email** button. Each new email replaces the previous link.
- Resending is rate limited — wait a few minutes between attempts.

## "This verification link is invalid or has already been used"

Each link works once. If you've already verified, you're done. If you requested a new email, only the newest link works.
