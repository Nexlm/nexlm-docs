# Chat and payment proof

Each trade has a private chat between the buyer and seller.

## Sending messages

- Messages up to 2,000 characters
- **Enter** sends, **Shift + Enter** adds a new line
- A "typing…" indicator shows when the other party is writing

## Attaching payment proof

Tap the image icon to attach a receipt.

| Rule | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Formats | JPEG, PNG, WebP |
| Size | 5 MB |

Files are checked by their actual content, not just their extension.

## System messages

Nexlm posts messages in the chat when escrow is locked, the buyer marks the trade as paid, and when XLM is released or refunded.

## How long chat stays open

You can keep chatting for 24 hours after a trade completes or is cancelled. After that the chat becomes read-only, but the history is kept.

::: danger
Nexlm staff will never ask for your password or OTP in chat. Keep all trade communication here — conversations on other apps can't be reviewed.
:::
