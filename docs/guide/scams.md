# Common scams

## Fake payment alerts

A buyer sends an SMS that looks like a bank credit, or an edited receipt, then pushes you to release.

**Protection:** confirm the credit in your banking app. Escrow keeps your XLM safe while you check.

## "Release first, I'll pay after"

No legitimate buyer needs this. Escrow exists precisely so nobody has to go first on trust.

## "Cancel and I'll send it directly"

The buyer asks you to cancel the trade and trade off-platform. You lose escrow protection completely.

## Third-party payments

Money arrives from someone other than the buyer — often stolen funds. The real account owner may later reverse the transfer or report you.

**Protection:** check the sender name matches the buyer's verified name.

## Overpayment refunds

The "buyer" sends more than agreed and asks you to refund the extra. The original payment is later reversed.

## Fake support

Someone claiming to be Nexlm support messages you asking for your password, OTP or to "verify" your wallet.

**Protection:** Nexlm never asks for these.

## What to do

- Don't release.
- Don't cancel on the other party's instruction.
- Keep chatting in the trade room so there's a record.
- Contact support with your trade ID.
