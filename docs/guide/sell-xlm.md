# Sell XLM

There are two ways to sell:

| Option | When to use |
| --- | --- |
| [Post a sell order](./post-order) | Set your own price and wait for buyers |
| Sell into a buy order | Sell immediately at a buyer's price |

## Selling into a buy order

1. Make sure you have [deposited XLM](./deposit-xlm) and added a [payout account](./payout-accounts) for the method you'll use.
2. On the market, switch to **Sell XLM**.
3. Choose a buyer and tap **Sell XLM**, then confirm.
4. Your XLM moves into escrow. You need the amount **plus 2 XLM** available.

## During the trade

1. Wait for the buyer to pay. They have 15 minutes.
2. When the buyer taps **I have paid**, check your bank or wallet app.
3. If the money is there, [release the XLM](./release).

::: danger Never release on a screenshot
Confirm the credit in your own banking app. SMS alerts and receipts can be faked.
:::

## If the buyer doesn't pay

When the 15-minute window closes without payment, the escrow refunds you automatically. If you were selling into your own posted order, it goes back on the market (unless it has expired).

::: tip
As a seller you can release at any point after escrow is locked — for example if the buyer paid but forgot to tap **I have paid**.
:::
