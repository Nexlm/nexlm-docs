# FAQ

## Does Nexlm hold my Naira?

No. Naira goes directly from the buyer's account to the seller's.

## Who controls the escrow?

Each escrow account's own key is disabled when it's created. Only the Nexlm platform key can co-sign, and the only transactions it signs send the XLM to the buyer or back to the seller.

## Can I see the escrow on the blockchain?

Yes. The trade room links to the lock, release and refund transactions on Stellar Expert.

## What if the seller never releases after I paid?

The XLM stays locked in escrow — it can't be refunded to the seller automatically once you've marked the trade as paid. Share your receipt in the chat and contact support with the trade ID. An in-app dispute process, where an admin reviews evidence and settles the escrow, is planned for Phase 2.

## Can I trade partially against a large order?

Not yet. A trade always takes the whole order. Partial fills are on the roadmap.

## Can I use my own Stellar wallet?

Not yet. Connecting an external wallet is planned.

## Is there a mobile app?

The web app works in mobile browsers. A native app is planned for Phase 3.

## Why is there a 2 XLM reserve per trade?

Stellar requires every account, including escrow accounts, to hold a minimum balance. See [Balances explained](./balances).

## How is the price decided?

By traders. Nexlm doesn't set rates.
