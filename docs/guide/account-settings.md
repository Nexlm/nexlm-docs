# Account settings

Open **Settings** from your avatar in the top bar.

## Account

Shows your display name, email and verification status, identity status, reputation, Stellar wallet address and join date.

## Phone number

Add a Nigerian mobile number. These formats are accepted and saved as `+234…`:

- `0803 123 4567`
- `08031234567`
- `+2348031234567`
- `2348031234567`

## Payout accounts

See [Add payout accounts](./payout-accounts).

## Change password

Enter your current password and a new one (at least 8 characters with a letter and a number). The new password must differ from the current one.

## Log out

Use the log-out icon in the top bar. Sessions also expire automatically after 7 days.
