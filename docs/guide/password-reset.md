# Reset your password

1. On the login page, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and tap **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and click the link. It's valid for **1 hour**.
4. Choose a new password and confirm it.

For privacy, Nexlm shows the same confirmation whether or not the email is registered.

## Link expired or already used

Request a new link. Only the most recent link works, and each link can be used once.

## Still locked out?

Contact support from the email address on your account.
