# Deposit XLM

1. Open **Wallet → Deposit**.
2. Copy your Stellar address or scan the QR code.
3. Send XLM from your exchange or wallet.

Deposits usually appear within seconds of the sending platform broadcasting the transaction. Tap **Refresh** on the Wallet page if needed.

## Checklist

- ✅ Coin: **XLM**
- ✅ Network: **Stellar**
- ✅ Address starts with **G** and matches the first and last characters shown in the app
- ✅ **No memo** needed — your Nexlm address is personal

::: danger Testnet vs mainnet
During early access the wallet is on Stellar **testnet**. The Deposit panel shows a warning when that's the case. Real XLM sent to a testnet address is not recoverable by Nexlm.
:::

## First deposit

If your wallet isn't activated yet, the first deposit must be at least **1 XLM**.

## Deposit not showing

1. Confirm the sending platform marked the withdrawal complete.
2. Look up its transaction hash on [Stellar Expert](https://stellar.expert) and check the destination address.
3. Refresh your Nexlm wallet.
